# Debug Dashboard Surface Brief

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Scope:** The complete `debug-dashboard` SPA, with the account workspace as its defining surface.
- **Audience and job:** A developer running the local mail sandbox needs to create, inspect, mutate, and delete mail state across Dovecot and Stalwart while understanding the resulting server evidence.
- **Task path:** Select a logical account, register one or both provider channels, perform a mailbox or account action, then inspect its provider-native receipt and correlated trace.
- **Proof and content:** Real service health, provider capabilities, mail state, operation progress, native identifiers, correlation confidence, and redacted raw logs.
- **Constraints:** Dense desktop operation, structural responsive collapse, keyboard access, reduced motion, familiar software controls, and no literal hardware controls or aviation jargon.
- **Chosen direction:** Flight-recorder workbench—recorder-paper work zones inside a graphite shell, Dovecot cyan and Stalwart amber channel markers, and a continuous contextual Trace lens.
- **Chosen composition:** Evidence Split—a roughly 62/38 mailbox/evidence workspace. Folders and messages occupy the left; the selected message, provider-native receipt, and vertical evidence inspector share the right; the chronological recorder strip spans the lower edge.
- **Memorable moment:** Moving one trace cursor aligns a selected message or operation with its Postfix, Dovecot, or Stalwart evidence without leaving the workspace.
- **Unresolved:** Exact open-source workhorse and monospace families and final density tokens.
